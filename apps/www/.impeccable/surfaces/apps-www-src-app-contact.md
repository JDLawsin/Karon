# Contact page

Mode: Persuade. Audience: a small-clinic dentist or assistant with a concrete question, concern, or workflow to share. Job: make direct contact feel low-friction and human. Primary action: open an email to the configured Karon contact address. Proof: the existing configured response promise and the same demo-request path already used across the site. Constraints: no invented availability, customer, or support claims; never invite patient details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Contact should feel like beginning one useful clinic conversation, not entering a sales funnel. It refuses the generic centered heading followed by a stack of equal contact cards.

OWN-WORLD: Karon white paper, Blue 600 field, Outfit, square-edged gray work surfaces, strong rules, and shared rectangular controls. No gradients, shadows, decorative icons, or dental imagery.

STORY: The visitor sees that any practical clinic question is welcome, learns what makes a useful first note, and chooses direct email or the existing guided demo route.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A desktop split field places the large contact promise and message anatomy on blue at left. A focused white email action occupies the right. Mobile collapses to the promise, then the email action, then guidance. The primary action is visible without scrolling.

FORM: Directly shaped from the user-supplied Calendly references: their focused split onboarding composition translated into Karon's visual language. No seed roll; the reference direction was explicit. Signature interaction is the native email handoff, with restrained control feedback only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Completion record

Evidence checked: `/contact` uses the promised blue-and-white split; the left side carries the practical clinic-conversation promise and useful-note anatomy; the right side opens a validated configured email when present and otherwise routes to the existing request flow.
