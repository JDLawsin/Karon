# Product surface

Mode: Persuade

Audience: A dentist running a small Philippine dental clinic. The page should make the live booking-to-chair workflow, its evidence, and the founding-clinic next step understandable within seconds.

Proof and constraints: Claims come from `content/claims.ts`; unfinished capabilities stay labeled; fake patient data is visibly identified; no public Karon list price appears while `docs/pricing.md` is draft; old capability, switching, and security URLs redirect here.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show Karon carrying a request into today’s chair in one continuous product story. Refuse the generic feature-card catalog and fragmented trust and switching pages.

OWN-WORLD: Use Karon’s white paper, Blue 600 structural fields, quiet gray workbench surfaces, Outfit, and square-to-soft rectangular controls. No gradients, glass, decorative shadows, or invented proof.

STORY: The dentist sees the request-to-inbox-to-Today path, verifies what is live, understands a respectful move from notebook and Messenger, reads plain security evidence, and applies or books a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Place a left-aligned booking-to-chair promise and two actions beside a large illustrative Today-board workflow. Keep the latest capability-check date and a visible fake-data label in the first viewport.

FORM: Established-world, code-led, product-led editorial sequence. This continued session recovered no original seed key or QUALITY BAR card; none was fabricated and the finished render is judged against `DESIGN.md` and the Karon craft floor.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
