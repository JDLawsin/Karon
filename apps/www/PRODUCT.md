# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The buyer is a dentist running a small dental clinic. The daily user is usually an
assistant or front-desk teammate working through a busy clinic day, often from a
phone and sometimes with unreliable connectivity.

## Product Purpose

Karon helps 1 to 2 chair dental clinics receive booking requests, review them in
one inbox, and run today's visits from one focused workspace. The marketing site
must help a dentist understand what is available now, what is still being shaped,
and whether a founding-clinic conversation is worthwhile.

## Positioning

Karon is dental-clinic software organized around the daily chair loop rather than
a broad hospital system. Public promises are generated from a claims registry and
unfinished capabilities stay visibly labeled.

## Operating Context

Small clinic teams commonly coordinate bookings and changes through a paper
notebook and Messenger. Karon's current live path is public booking request to
booking inbox to Today board. Existing-record migration is not yet a self-serve
capability and must remain labeled as a preview.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public Product page consolidates the previous capability, switching, and
  security pages into one persuasive route.
- Live capabilities and security controls come from `content/claims.ts`.
- Building and absent capabilities must never be presented as shipped.
- No invented customer counts, testimonials, compliance badges, roadmap dates,
  pricing, or security guarantees.
- Old public URLs should redirect to the Product page instead of breaking.

## Brand Commitments

Karon uses Outfit, Blue 600 as its primary action color, white paper, quiet gray
workbench surfaces, and the shared Karon wordmark. The Product page uses the
provided Calendly screenshots as a reference for product-led pacing, large
interface demonstrations, and clear section rhythm without copying Calendly's
identity, decorative gradients, or claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- The public claims registry is `content/claims.ts`.
- Existing product, switching, and security copy lives in `content/features.ts`,
  `content/switching.ts`, and `content/security.ts`.
- Visual references are in `product/launch/Calendly Web Onboarding/` and
  `product/launch/calendly/`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, or approved quantitative outcome claims are
  available for this page.

## Product Principles

1. Show the product doing the work before listing capabilities.
2. Keep shipped and unfinished work unmistakably separate.
3. Make the move from notebook and Messenger feel respectful and manageable.
4. Explain security in plain language without compliance theater.
5. Give every section one clear next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The marketing site targets WCAG 2.2 AA, supports keyboard navigation and visible
focus, maintains at least 44px touch targets, and must work without horizontal page
scroll from 320px upward.
